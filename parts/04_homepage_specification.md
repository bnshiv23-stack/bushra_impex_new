# ============================================
# HOMEPAGE SPECIFICATION
# ============================================

The homepage is the most important page of the website.

This page should not feel like an agricultural catalogue.

It should feel like the digital headquarters of one of India's largest agricultural equipment brands.

The visitor should immediately think:

"This company looks established."

"This brand looks premium."

"These machines must be high quality."

The homepage should focus 60% on the brand.

40% on products.

The homepage should be approximately 10–12 sections long.

Every section should have generous whitespace.

Never overload a section with too much information.

Every section should tell one story.
